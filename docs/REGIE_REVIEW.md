## Finding

The branch captures controlled dialogue, pauses and a quid-pro-quo mood. It does
not implement the requested cooperative, enduring rescue investigation. This is
a product-direction mismatch, not something solved with bloom or more particles.

carefully authored prose are useful. They are retained as a comparison, not
mistaken for an adequate complete game.

## What this change actually implements

A playable **local director's review**, deliberately not the production game:

- Existing black-coat / circular-glass / green-torch media, unchanged binaries.
- A persistent room instead of a small visual followed by an endless transcript.
- Multiple topics and repeat-question memory; a refused bargain does not block progress.
- Observation cards with explicit source identity; two copies do not become two witnesses.
- Separate operations for disclosure, challenge and counterproof. Presenting a
  private observation does not automatically release it to the council.
- Performed anger under pressure; the genuine breakdown only after an explicitly
  disputed statement is refuted by two independent observed sources.
- Persistent aftermath and a narrow real win, without claiming the animals are
  rescued or that every loss was secretly intended.
- Replay, malformed-storage handling, duplicate/stale callback rejection and a
  memory-only fallback. Complete gameplay with reduced motion or failed video.

All demonstration observations are labelled fictitious. The other witness is
explicitly simulated. There are no hidden network participants and no live game
writes. This slice does not claim AI understanding of unrestricted free text.

## The complete event: what must follow in the private JGA app

### 1. The world changes before anyone opens the cell

Use the existing world-event state to expose web-filled stalls and the genuinely
missing, recoverable mounts. The capture is real. Give each affected participant
an authenticated, individual assignment; use actual mount/shapechanger rules
rather than inventing a common animal for everyone. Steven's exception must be
noticed, not silently flattened into the other disappearances.

### 2. Make dialogue an investigation, not a reward dispenser

Every exchange should have an intention and a cost in **in-game** information:
ask a precise question, offer an observation, reveal a suspicion, decline a
bargain, challenge a statement, link evidence, or hold something under seal.
Moriondo can omit, redirect and manipulate. His claims have stable IDs so that
players can confront their actual content later. He responds to what the player
has done in the game, not invented knowledge of their private life.

Authored facts and the truth graph stay on the server. A language layer may
interpret an utterance or phrase a reply only within the currently allowed
intent/evidence frame. It must never award truth, invent evidence, disclose the
complete graph or advance authoritative state. Unknown intent should prompt a
specific clarification in character rather than a false success. Keep a fully
playable explicit-action alternative.

### 3. Cooperation is necessary, not ornamental

Different recipients hold genuinely different observations. A released clue is
visible, not automatically endorsed. Corroboration needs independent origin and
appropriate role access, not two votes or two repeated cards. A participant can
contest the conclusion without withdrawing the underlying observation.

Build the dependency graph privately around **Brut, Pfad, Hort, Schnitt**.
Server-side readiness requires the four evidence-backed elements and the proper
transmission role; Steven's spider is necessary but cannot manufacture the
missing deduction. No single player should receive the whole answer in one visit.

### 4. A visible adversary with an actual dramatic memory

Map accepted server events to semantic media cues, not arbitrary timer shuffling.
New evidence can stop pacing. An offered bargain can bring him to the glass.
An unsupported accusation can earn performed anger. The independently proven
failure of his command earns the genuine rage break once. Its aftermath persists
across reloads, subsequent visits and different participating devices.

Keep transitions pose-aware. Preserve whole-body framing and approved identity.
Use matching pre-approved dialogue audio only; do not relabel old audio as the new
script. Text must remain accessible when sound or motion is off. Silence may build
tension, but should never conceal an arbitrary inaccessible timing requirement.

### 5. Escalate through evidence, not a fixed quiz count

Visits should reopen after meaningful new information, not repeat identical tasks.
Wrong hypotheses create an intelligible contradiction to investigate, not a
permanent lost mount or brute-force-friendly unlimited reward loop. Hints become
more concrete without silently solving the last inference. Support absent players,
late arrivals, repeat visits, host recovery and an auditable correction path.

The four-part counter-command produces real mount rescue. The larger unassigned
cocoon is a separate discovery; its early frames must not identify Jan. The
approved Phial/three-smith structure resolves the ancient binding only after the
proper collective gates. Preserve the victory when the next cycle opens.

## Production acceptance gate

A release needs two real authenticated devices demonstrating private clue
separation, intentional sharing, independent corroboration, persistent disagreement,
server-validated resolution and scoped media frames. Test retries and concurrent
moves, role impersonation, stale evidence, request tampering, disconnect/reconnect,
late participants, locked reveals and host recovery. No answer graph or future
private clue data in the public JS bundle, HTML or API response. A green local
regie test is not proof that these production requirements are met.

This delivery opens a review branch only. It does not merge or deploy production.
